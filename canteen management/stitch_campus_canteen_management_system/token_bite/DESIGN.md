## Brand & Style

The design system powers an elevated dining and high-throughput order execution ecosystem engineered for campus life. It bridges high-pace collegiate utility with culinary prestige. The aesthetic avoids institutional cafeteria paradigms, embracing instead a tactile, contemporary culinary editorial vibe reminiscent of premium fast-casual eateries and modern Scandinavian lifestyle brands, synthesized with the surgical clarity of Apple interface principles.

### Visual Philosophy & Aesthetic Tenets
- **Warm Editorial Functionalism:** High typographic rhythm pairs with rich, organic surfaces. Food is treated as craft, while queueing and order tracking are treated with precision data mechanics.
- **Physicality and Tactical Affirmation:** Micro-elevations, cushioned press states, distinct tokens, and real-time state steppers mimic physical counter tickets and illuminated order boards.
- **Atmospheric Warmth:** Surfaces reject clinical blue-whites in favor of warm stone, biscuit, and obsidian backplates that make food imagery pop and reduce sensory fatigue in high-stress campus environments.
- **Speed Under Pressure:** High-contrast glanceability built for bustling, noisy hall environments where students sprint between lectures and kitchen operators oversee dynamic ticket queues.

## Colors

The system uses a chromatic hierarchy built around culinary warmth, operational clarity, and high-visibility status cues.

### Core Role Assignments
- **Primary (`#E87532` — Food Orange):** Reserved for forward actions, active cart states, primary checkout pathways, and focal point order progression indicators.
- **Secondary (`#4F7C59` — Fresh Green):** Denotes operational readiness, active kitchen stations, pickup tokens ready for collection, healthy dietary tags, and positive stock affirmations.
- **Tertiary (`#DDD3C2` — Muted Beige):** Structural mediator. Anchors container borders, divider lines, inset trays, subtle tag fills, and inactive control backgrounds.
- **Neutral (`#171717` — Deep Charcoal):** Primary text, high-priority headings, solid state active pills, and high-contrast night-mode surfaces.

### Semantic & Supportive Roles
- **Canvas Base (`#F7F3EA` — Warm Cream):** The foundational daylight surface. Provides an appetite-inducing background that softens contrast compared to stark pure white.
- **Surface Elevation (`#FFFFFF` — Card White):** Strictly applied to interactive cards, sheets, elevated modals, and food item containers to guarantee photorealistic food rendering.
- **Critical Alert (`#DC2626` — Alert Red):** Strictly isolated for system errors, out-of-stock items, kitchen closures, order cancellations, and severe dietary allergen warnings. Never used decoratively.

### Dark Mode Architecture (Warm Obsidian)
- Dark mode completely avoids pure `#000000`. Base canvasing shifts to warm volcanic obsidian (`#121212`), while cards elevate to rich layered charcoal (`#1A1A1A` and `#242424`).
- Borders transition from Muted Beige to translucent warm smoke (`rgba(221, 211, 194, 0.12)`).
- Food Orange shifts slightly in luminance for dark backgrounds (`#F08344`), while Fresh Green recalibrates to an illuminated mint-sage (`#5E946A`) to maintain WCAG AAA legibility.

## Typography

Typographic hierarchy balances three distinct typefaces to separate dramatic branding, tactical glanceability, and high-density readability.

### Typeface Roles
- **Sora (Structural Display & Counters):** Delivers clean geometric mass for large meal names, category headers, live ETA counters, and order tokens (e.g., `#A-142`). Its wide-aperture geometry guarantees quick readability from distance on kitchen displays or across crowded counters.
- **Plus Jakarta Sans (Interactive Labels & Metadata):** Fuels UI navigation, button microcopy, active canteen status flags, tags, and dietary labels. Its balanced terminals bring energetic friendliness while retaining executive polish.
- **Inter (Engineered Body & Data Lists):** Handles ingredients, nutritional breakdowns, transaction receipts, allergen disclaimers, and kitchen modifier tickets with tabular numerals enabled (`tnum`).

## Layout & Spacing

The layout system is mobile-first, prioritizing one-handed thumb interaction during transit across campus, while smoothly expanding into dense multi-column ticket views for kitchen dispatch desks and tablet POS terminals.

### Grid & Breakpoints
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column fluid layout with `1rem` margins and `1rem` gutters. All primary action hubs (checkout, cart sheets, active token alerts) pin to a sticky bottom thumb safe zone (minimum height `56px` + safe area inset).
- **Tablet (768px – 1024px):** 8-column layout with `2rem` margins and `1.5rem` gutters. Splits menu browsing and ongoing order progression into persistent dual panes.
- **Desktop / Kitchen Display (> 1024px):** 12-column layout capped at `1440px` max-width. Employs a kanban column architecture for kitchen operations (New Orders → Preparing → Ready for Pickup → Archived) utilizing `2rem` gutters.

### Touch Ergonomics & Vertical Rhythm
- Maintain a strict 4px/8px incremental base rhythm.
- Action items within handheld mobile views strictly respect a `48px` minimum interactive touch perimeter.
- Spacing between related food card elements relies on `space-sm` (8px), while distinct culinary categories separate by `space-xl` (40px).

## Elevation & Depth

Depth is established through physical, architectural stacking rather than artificial heavy drop shadows, reinforcing a tactile paper-ticket and cafeteria tray metaphor.

### Layering Architecture
- **Layer 0 (Canvas Base):** Warm Cream (`#F7F3EA`) in light mode; Deep Obsidian (`#121212`) in dark mode. Receded, static substrate.
- **Layer 1 (Resting Cards & Trays):** Pure White (`#FFFFFF`) or Charcoal (`#1A1A1A`) featuring a crisp 1px structural stroke using Muted Beige (`#DDD3C2`) or Dark Smoke (`rgba(255,255,255,0.08)`).
- **Layer 2 (Interactive Floating Elements & Food Quick-Picks):** Supported by warm ambient shadows: `box-shadow: 0 4px 16px -2px rgba(23, 23, 23, 0.06), 0 2px 4px -1px rgba(23, 23, 23, 0.03)`.
- **Layer 3 (Overlays, Bottom Trays, & Kitchen Modals):** Tinted soft diffusion: `box-shadow: 0 12px 32px -4px rgba(23, 23, 23, 0.12), 0 4px 8px -2px rgba(23, 23, 23, 0.04)`.
- **Layer 4 (Live Order Tokens & Sticky Status Bars):** High-prominence micro-border paired with warm-tinted orange or green edge glow to pull immediate peripheral attention without distracting from primary tasks.

## Shapes

The interface employs a rounded geometric profile (`level 2`) to balance soft campus warmth with structured industrial ordering cards.

### Shape Geometry Rules
- **Base Components (Inputs, Small Badges, Quantity Selectors):** `0.5rem` (8px) radius. Ensures high structural definition.
- **Mid-Tier Elements (Food Cards, Counter Alerts, Dialogue Sheets):** `1rem` (16px) radius (`rounded-lg`). Creates a friendly, handheld tray feel.
- **Large Panels & Bottom Drawers:** `1.5rem` (24px) radius on top edges (`rounded-xl`).
- **Interactive State Chips & Operational Badges:** Full-pill execution (`rounded-full` / `9999px`) to maintain clear differentiation from rectangular meal cards and tabular operational data blocks.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Order Button:** Food Orange background (`#E87532`), crisp white typography (`#FFFFFF`, Sora/Plus Jakarta Sans bold). Subtly scales down (`scale(0.98)`) on press with an immediate tactile spring curve.
- **Secondary / Add Action:** Card White background, 1.5px Muted Beige outline, Deep Charcoal text. On hover/active, transitions to Warm Cream fill with an Orange border.
- **Kitchen Action Trigger:** Solid Fresh Green (`#4F7C59`) for "Mark Ready" or Deep Charcoal for "Print Slip".

### Chips & Operational Badges
- **Live Kitchen Status Badge:** Pill shape. In-progress states use soft Fresh Green tint (`rgba(79, 124, 89, 0.12)`) with solid Green text and a pulsating 6px circular green status beacon.
- **Dietary & Category Chips:** Warm Cream or White backing with Muted Beige border. Active selected state shifts to solid Deep Charcoal with white text.

### Food & Kitchen Cards
- **Canteen Menu Card:** White surface, subtle 1px border. Features a 1:1 or 4:3 rounded thumbnail, bold Sora title, price tag in numeric bold, and a fixed bottom thumb-accessible "+" counter button.
- **Kitchen Ticket Display Card:** Tabular layout with high-contrast token header, time elapsed badge, ordered items checklist with strike-through support, and clear allergy flags bordered in Alert Red.

### Token Number Display (#A-142)
- High-visibility hero element. Set in Sora Extra Bold. Styled inside a ticket-stub container with perforated dashed divider borders and a high-contrast illuminated status halo (Green when ready for collection, Orange when being cooked).

### Order Status Stepper
- Horizontal continuous track connecting four key milestones: *Received → Kitchen Prep → Ready at Counter → Collected*.
- Solid Green progress bar with animated active pulses on the current station node. Inactive stations use Muted Beige tracking lines.

### Inputs & Number Steppers
- **Search & Special Instructions:** Inset warm background (`#F7F3EA` in light mode) with subtle inner shadow, transitioning to crisp white background and a 1.5px Food Orange border when focused.
- **Quantity Selector:** Compact, tactile pill segment housing `-` and `+` symbols separated by bold centered tabular values, preventing accidental multi-tap triggers via strict 44px tap targets.
